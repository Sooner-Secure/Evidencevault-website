LEGAL / PRIVACY

Privacy policy.

Evidence Vault collects no app data. This policy explains what stays in your project file and how this separate website and support email work.

EFFECTIVE NOVEMBER 9, 2026
SOONER SECURE

Who we are

Evidence Vault is developed by Sooner Secure, an Oklahoma nonprofit. Questions or privacy requests about Evidence Vault can be sent to pat@soonersecure.org.

No app data collection

Evidence Vault does not collect information about you, your app usage, or your projects. The app does not send your imported evidence, project files, passwords, or chain of custody records to Sooner Secure. There is no app analytics or telemetry.

Evidence Vault saves each project as a database file protected by the password you choose. Files you add to a project and its chain of custody records are part of that project. The app lets you export a full project or individual files with their associated chain of custody document. You choose when to create, import, and export this material.

This website has no evidence upload feature and does not receive your project files through a visit to the site. Website hosting and email support are separate from the app’s no-data-collection design. Handle exported files and passwords according to your organization’s own security practices.

Website visits

The website’s page code does not currently use analytics, advertising trackers, cookies, or browser storage. Depending on how you host the site, a hosting provider may use information needed to authenticate visitors if access is restricted. The provider may also process standard technical information, such as IP address, request time, requested pages, and security events, to deliver and protect the site.

Support messages

If you email support, we receive the address you send from, your message, and any information you choose to include. We use that information to respond and to keep an appropriate record of the request. Please avoid sending passwords or sensitive evidence by email.

App Store and other sites

Apple handles App Store purchases under its own privacy practices. Links from this website to Sooner Secure and other services take you to sites with their own policies. See Sooner Secure’s privacy notice for its broader organizational practices.

Sharing, retention, and requests

We do not sell personal information. Website hosting and email providers may process information needed to provide those services. We keep support correspondence only as long as reasonably needed for support, recordkeeping, or legal obligations.

Depending on where you live, you may be able to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information we hold about you. Contact pat@soonersecure.org and we will respond as required by applicable law.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy if the app, website, or its providers change. The effective date above will show when this page was last updated.
